ESSENTIAL IMMIGRATION KNOWLEDGE

Good to Know Before You Go.

Quick, bite-sized answers to critical administrative questions every applicant should know.

What is the difference between a visitor visa and a work visa?

A visitor visa grants temporary recreational or family visitation rights with a strict ban on employment. A work permit explicitly authorizes paid employment with specific employer sponsorship.

Why is financial documentation so strictly audited?

Embassies need verified assurance that applicants can sustain living expenses and tuition without illegally accessing public welfare funds or unauthorized employment.

How should you prepare for a consular visa interview?

Be concise, honest, and consistent with your submitted dossier. Clearly articulate your academic or career goals and establish ties that guarantee departure.

Where can you find official, up-to-date visa requirements?

Always cross-check official sovereign portals (e.g. Canada.ca, Gov.uk, Homeaffairs.gov.au). Vayora monitors these ministerial gazettes in real-time.
