FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Answers to Your Destination Questions.

Direct guidance on country selection, eligibility benchmarking, and international relocation.

The ideal destination depends on your academic goals, budget, and long-term residency plans. Canada and Australia offer the most structured post-study work permits (up to 3 to 4 years) with direct pathways to permanent residency. Germany provides tuition-free education at public universities with an 18-month job seeker visa. The UK offers prestigious 1-year Master's programs coupled with a 2-year Graduate Route work visa, while the US leads in STEM OPT opportunities extending up to 3 years.

While each sovereign country enforces specific statutory checklists, standard relocation dossiers require:
  • Valid international passport with at least 6 months validity beyond your planned stay.
  • Academic credential assessment (e.g., WES for Canada, ACS/VETASSESS for Australia).
  • Official language test scorecards (IELTS General/Academic, PTE, or TOEFL).
  • Proof of financial solvency (unencumbered bank statements, sponsorship affidavits).
  • National police clearance certificates and immigration medical examination reports.
  • Detailed employment reference letters aligned to national NOC / ANZSCO occupation codes.

Germany permits 140 full days or 280 half days of employment per year. The US allows on-campus work up to 20 hours per week, with Curricular Practical Training (CPT) for off-campus internships.

Visa eligibility is primarily calculated through age, formal educational qualifications, certified language proficiency, and skilled work experience. Canada and Australia utilize transparent points-based grids (Comprehensive Ranking System for Canada, SkillSelect Points Test for Australia). European nations like Germany emphasize direct degree-recognition and job offers matching salary thresholds, while the UK requires Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) from a licensed Home Office sponsor. Overview & Relocation Advantages

Canada remains one of the world's most sought-after immigration destinations, welcoming over 450,000 new permanent residents annually across Federal Express Entry, Provincial Nominee Programs (PNPs), and international graduate pathways.

Popular Visa Categories & Eligibility
  • Express Entry (Federal Skilled Worker): Points-based selection evaluated on age, education (ECA), IELTS CLB 7+, and continuous skilled experience.
  • Provincial Nominee Program (PNP): 600 bonus CRS points nominated by provinces like Ontario, BC, and Alberta matching local labor market demands.
  • Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP): Open work permit granted for 1 to 3 years following graduation from a Canadian Designated Learning Institution.
