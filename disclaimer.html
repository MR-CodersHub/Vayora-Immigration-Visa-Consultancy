Statutory Separation Notice

Vayora is a private commercial visa and immigration consultancy. Vayora is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an agency of any sovereign government, embassy, high commission, or diplomatic consulate.

1. Independent Advisory Status

Vayora operates as an independent consultancy offering professional profile assessment, document compilation, translation verification, and strategic lodgement guidance. Clients have the sovereign legal right to apply directly to government immigration portals (such as IRCC Canada, Australia Home Affairs, UK Visas and Immigration, and US Consulates) without engaging an intermediary or third-party consultancy.

2. No Guarantee of Visa Grant

All decisions concerning the grant, refusal, delay, or security screening of any visa, travel authorization, or residency permit are made exclusively by consular officers and sovereign immigration departments under their national laws.

Vayora does not possess, represent, or claim authority to expedite, influence, or guarantee the outcome of any visa application. Our role is strictly limited to ensuring that client dossiers conform to established legal requirements and documentation benchmarks.

3. Informational Nature of Guides & Tools

All country guides, processing time estimates, CRS points calculators, and cost-of-living metrics published on this website are compiled for informational and educational purposes only. Sovereign immigration policies, quota limits, occupation lists, and minimum financial thresholds are subject to immediate revision by national governments without prior notice.

Users must verify current statutory fees and criteria with official sovereign publications or during an individualized consultation with a licensed advisor.

4. Professional Fees vs. Government Levies

Fees paid to Vayora represent professional casework retainers for legal strategy, documentation audits, and case management. These retainers are completely independent of statutory consular application charges, biometric processing fees, medical assessment costs, or translation disbursements payable directly to destination authorities.

5. Third-Party Websites & Consular Links

This website may contain hyperlinks to official foreign government portals, university admissions pages, language testing organizations (IELTS, PTE, TOEFL), and credential evaluation agencies (WES, ICAS).
