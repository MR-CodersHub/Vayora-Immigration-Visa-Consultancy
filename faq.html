No consultancy, attorney, or advisor can ethically guarantee visa issuance. Sovereign governments (such as IRCC Canada, Home Affairs Australia, UK Home Office, and US Dept of State) hold sole constitutional authority to adjudicate applications. However, Vayora ensures an industry-leading 98% dossier compliance standard by mitigating common refusal triggers such as financial ambiguity, incomplete documentation, or profile misalignments.

Yes, most top educational destinations permit international students to work part-time during active academic terms and full-time during scheduled vacations. For example:
  • Canada: Up to 20-24 hours per week off-campus during terms.
  • Australia: Up to 48 hours per fortnight.
  • United Kingdom: Up to 20 hours per week for degree-level studies.
  • Germany: Up to 140 full days or 280 half days per calendar year.

Graduating from an accredited Designated Learning Institution provides lucrative post-graduation work opportunities:
  • Canada: Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) valid for up to 3 years.
  • Australia: Subclass 485 Temporary Graduate Visa granting 2 to 4 years.
  • United Kingdom: Graduate Route Visa offering 2 years (3 years for PhDs).
  • Germany: 18-Month Jobseeker Visa with right to convert to EU Blue Card.

Canada operates Express Entry (Federal Skilled Worker, Canadian Experience Class, and Provincial Nominee Programs - PNP). Australia manages General Skilled Migration (Subclass 189 Skilled Independent, Subclass 190 Skilled Nominated, and Subclass 491 Regional). New Zealand utilizes the Skilled Migrant Category. Our consultants run comprehensive points calculations based on your age, education, English proficiency, and work credentials.

The Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card) is Germany's modernized points-based 1-year residence permit allowing non-EU skilled professionals to search for employment locally while permitted to work up to 20 hours/week. Once you obtain an eligible employment contract, you can directly transition into an EU Blue Card, leading to permanent residency in as fast as 21 to 27 months.

Proof of Funds demonstrates that you possess sufficient liquid, unencumbered finances to cover living expenses, tuition fees, or initial settlement without relying on public assistance. Required amounts vary by destination:
  • Canada: ~$20,635 CAD for single students or ~$14,690 CAD for Express Entry single applicants.
  • Germany: €11,904 in a mandatory blocked account (Sperrkonto).
  • UK: Living maintenance funds of £1,023/month (or £1,334/month in London).
Vayora assists in structuring legally compliant bank audit certificates and source-of-wealth declarations.

Most sovereign authorities accept IELTS Academic or General Training, PTE Academic / PTE Core, and TOEFL iBT. Canada now accepts PTE Core for Express Entry economic streams, while the UK requires UKVI-approved SELT tests for specific visas. Vayora conducts pre-tests and advises on target band requirements for maximum CRS/points generation.
