TRUST & CONFIDENTIALITY

Privacy Policy.

How Vayora collects, safeguards, and processes your sensitive immigration data in compliance with international privacy frameworks.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Standard: GDPR, PIPEDA & Privacy Act Compliant Print Policy

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

At Vayora Immigration & Visa Consultancy LLP ("Vayora", "we", "us", or "our"), safeguarding your personal and legal records is a foundational responsibility. We recognize that international visa applications necessitate the submission of sensitive identification, financial solvency, police clearance, and academic records. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data management standards and your legal privacy entitlements.

2. Information We Collect

To evaluate eligibility, compile visa dossiers, and interface with sovereign portals, we collect:

  • Biographical & Identity Data: Full legal names, passport details, nationality, date of birth, photographs, and marital status.
  • Contact Details: Verified email addresses, telephone numbers, residential history, and emergency contact details.
  • Professional & Educational Records: Degrees, transcripts, employment reference letters, CVs, and language test scores (IELTS/PTE/TOEFL).
  • Financial Proofs: Bank statements, property valuations, tax returns, and sponsorship affidavits required by sovereign visa authorities.
  • Statutory Clearance Records: Certified police conduct certificates and authorized panel physician medical examination receipts.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

Vayora processes personal data strictly under lawful bases recognized by international data protection regulations:

  • Performance of a Contract: Processing is essential to execute our immigration advisory agreement and lodge your application.
  • Explicit Client Consent: For handling sensitive documents such as medical clearance certificates and financial records.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Adhering to statutory record-keeping mandates imposed by licensed regulatory bodies (RCIC, MARA, OISC).

4. Data Security & Encryption Standards

All electronic files uploaded to the Vayora Client Dashboard Vault are secured using industry-leading technical measures:

  • Encryption at Rest & in Transit: End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption for data transfer and military-grade AES-256 bit encryption for storage.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Only your designated licensed counsel and assigned caseworkers can inspect your dossier.
  • Dual-Tier Audit Logging: Every document download, inspection, or status change is cryptographically timestamped in our immutable audit log.

5. Authorized Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or commercialize your personal information. Disclosures occur strictly with your prior authorization to:

  • Official sovereign immigration departments (e.g., IRCC Canada, Home Office UK, Department of Home Affairs Australia, USCIS).
  • Authorized credential assessment bodies (WES, ACS, VETASSESS, Engineers Australia).
  • Official biometric collection and visa application centers (VFS Global, TLScontact).

6. Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the statutory right to:

  • Request a full export of your personal information stored in our vaults.
  • Request rectification of inaccurate biographical or employment records.
  • Request data erasure following the conclusion of your statutory retention window.
  • Withdraw consent for optional advisory newsletters or alerts at any moment.

7. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

For privacy inquiries, data subject access requests (DSAR), or regulatory feedback, reach out directly to our Data Protection Officer:
