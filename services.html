Timelines vary significantly depending on the destination nation, visa category, and seasonal embassy volume. For example, Canadian study permits typically take 4–8 weeks, UK Skilled Worker visas 3–6 weeks, and Australian skilled migration streams 3–9 months. During your initial strategy session, we provide an accurate, country-specific processing timeline forecast.

A work permit (such as an LMIA-supported Canadian work permit or UK Skilled Worker visa) grants you temporary status tied to a specific employer or authorized employment period. Permanent Residency (PR), such as Canada Express Entry or Australia Subclass 189, grants you the permanent right to live, work, and access state healthcare anywhere in the host nation without sponsorship ties.
