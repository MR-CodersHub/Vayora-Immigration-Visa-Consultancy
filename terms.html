LEGAL & SERVICE AGREEMENT

Terms of Service

Standard Terms of Engagement governing professional immigration advisory services, client representations, fee schedules, and mutual responsibilities.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 4.2 (Standard Global Engagement)
Print Terms

1. Scope of Advisory Services

Vayora Immigration & Visa Consultancy provides independent, regulated professional advisory, profile feasibility assessment, document audit, and application filing assistance for international migration, higher education admissions, and temporary foreign work visas across Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom, the United States, Germany, and New Zealand.

Our services encompass legal eligibility review, guidance on sovereign immigration statutes, strategic dossier collation, certified translation verification, and scheduling of consular appointments.

2. Non-Affiliation with Sovereign Consulates

Vayora is a private international immigration advisory firm and is not an agency, branch, or representative of any government or sovereign consular department (including Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), the Australian Department of Home Affairs, the UK Home Office, or the US Department of State).

Important Notice: Sovereign governments reserve the exclusive constitutional right and sole discretion to grant, deny, delay, or review any visa application. No immigration consultancy or attorney can guarantee a visa approval.

3. Client Obligations & Truthful Disclosures

The client expressly covenants and warrants that:

  • All biographical details, employment histories, academic credentials, and financial solvency statements provided to Vayora are genuine, accurate, and complete.
  • No forged, manipulated, or counterfeit documents will be submitted at any stage of advisory or government lodgement.
  • The client will promptly notify their designated Vayora consultant regarding any prior visa refusals, deportations, criminal convictions, or changes in marital status or civil health records.
  • The client acknowledges that submitting false representation to sovereign authorities (misrepresentation) will result in immediate termination of the advisory retainer without refund, and may trigger multi-year sovereign entry bans.

4. Professional Advisory Retainers & Milestones

Fees payable to Vayora represent professional casework retainers for legal analysis, dossier preparation, and application monitoring. Fees are structured on a transparent, milestone-based schedule as documented in the individual Client Service Contract:

  • Phase 1: Initial Feasibility & Dossier Setup — Due upon retainer contract execution.
  • Phase 2: Document Verification & Translation Audit — Due prior to official government portal drafting.
  • Phase 3: Sovereign Submission Lodgement — Due prior to final consular submission and biometric booking.

Statutory Government Fees: Sovereign government processing charges, visa sticker fees, biometrics levies, IELTS/PTE examination costs, police clearances, and authorized medical examination fees are statutory third-party disbursements payable directly to respective authorities and are separate from Vayora advisory retainers.

5. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Vayora maintains an ethical, fair refund policy rooted in our Milestone Completion Index:

  • Cancellation within 7 calendar days of enrollment: Full refund of advisory retainers minus a standard non-refundable file onboarding fee of $150 USD, provided profile documentation has not commenced.
  • Cancellation after Stage 1 (Initial Feasibility): 70% refund of total contract fees.
  • Cancellation after Stage 2 (Document Assembly & Legal Drafting): 30% refund of contract fees.
  • Cancellation after Official Government Portal Lodgement (Stage 3): Professional casework is fully performed; no refund is applicable once official submission is lodged with the destination government.

6. Confidentiality & Security of Records

All documentation uploaded to the Vayora Client Portal is secured with end-to-end 256-bit AES encryption in compliance with international privacy protocols (GDPR, PIPEDA). Documents are retained exclusively for the duration of the migration process and archived or purged in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

7. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Vayora, its directors, employees, and authorized agents shall not be liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from sovereign policy modifications, legislative quota reductions, diplomatic visa backlog extensions, or consular rejections based on sovereign discretion.

8. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These Terms of Service and any individual Client Advisory Contracts shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the jurisdiction specified in the signed Retainer Agreement. Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising under this agreement shall be settled through good-faith mediation prior to formal arbitration.
